## Summary

PhD student in experimental high-energy physics at the University of Alabama and member of the CMS Collaboration at CERN's Large Hadron Collider, advised by Dr. Emanuele Usai. My research covers the search for four-top-quark production in Run 3, testing and data-quality monitoring for the CMS High Granularity Calorimeter (HGCAL) upgrade, and machine learning for detector data-quality monitoring. Alongside research, I train physicists in research software: I am on the training teams of HSF and IRIS-HEP, a certified Carpentries instructor, a facilitator at the CMS Data Analysis School, and I have taught, mentored or organized more than 25 workshops and courses for audiences from high school students and STEM teachers in Puerto Rico to HEP postdocs.
